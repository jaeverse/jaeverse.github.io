What I do

From business requirement to a live warehouse floor

I turn what a warehouse needs into how its system behaves. And when something doesn't behave, whether it's on the RF Gun, in a WebWise page or deep in a back-end workflow, I trace it back through the process and the data, and ship the hotfix.

RF Gun noun The handheld barcode scanner warehouse operators carry. It talks to the WMS over a wireless (radio frequency) network, so every scan of a pallet, bin or item updates the system in real time.

Advantage Architect

Where system behaviour is shaped, mostly on the RF terminal: the screens, prompts and checks operators work through every shift.

I build: business processes for RF handheld workflows

WebWise Page Editor

Web pages in HighJump / Körber / Infios that pull live data from the database and let users act on it.

I build: pages and reports to user specs

T-SQL underneath

The stored procedures behind RF screens, pick/put rules, allocation and putaway. Where the real logic lives.

I write and tune them

What I developed

Shipped to real warehouse floors

A few of the processes I've designed and built, end to end.

  1. DEV-01 RF · Voice

    Hybrid Scan + Voice Pick

    Batch and case picking that combines barcode scans with voice-directed prompts, so operators keep their eyes on the shelf and their hands on the stock.

  2. DEV-02 Architect · T-SQL

    Publisher / Subscriber in Advantage Architect

    Business processes that T-SQL can call directly, so logic in the database can hand work over to the WMS.

  3. DEV-03 Allocation

    Pick/put rules for transfer orders

    Item allocation by expiry date, so the stock that expires first leaves first, plus the picking flow that follows it.

  4. DEV-04 Kitting

    Move to Stock

    The kitting process that moves finished kits into stock, ready to be allocated and picked.

  5. DEV-05 Inventory

    Merge MLP

    Combines two MLPs (master license plates) into one, so the system matches the pallet on the floor.
